We Believe in Nurturing Every Talent

Education is just the beginning. At Pinnacle, we prioritise sports, arts, and all-round activities to help every child discover their true potential.

Sports First, Always

We believe physical education and sports are as important as academics. Our curriculum gives equal priority to sports, arts, robotics, and cultural activities — because a child's growth is measured beyond the classroom. We support every child's passion, whether it's cricket, football, drawing, or dancing.

Chairman's Message

"

Dear Students and Parents,

Welcome to Pinnacle Upper Primary School — a place where every child is celebrated, every talent is nurtured, and every dream is encouraged. Our vision is to provide holistic, value-based education that goes beyond textbooks and prepares children for life.

At Pinnacle, we believe that true education is the harmonious development of the head, heart, and hands. We are committed to academic excellence, character building, and creating an environment where curiosity thrives and every child feels safe, supported, and inspired.

Together, let us build a brighter future — one child at a time.

Mohammed Abdul Khais
